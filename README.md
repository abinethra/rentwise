RentWise is a full-stack web app that helps tenants understand rental agreements.
Upload a PDF or a photo of your agreement and RentWise reviews it clause by clause,
rates each clause as low, medium or high risk, explains it in plain English, and
points out important protections that are missing, such as a deposit refund timeline
or a fair notice period. It then drafts polite messages you can send to your
landlord to negotiate the risky clauses.

Many tenants sign agreements without fully understanding them, which can lead to
unfair deposits, lock-in periods and eviction terms. RentWise makes that
information easy to read and act on.

> ⚠️ RentWise provides guidance, not legal advice. For serious disputes,
> consult a qualified lawyer.
